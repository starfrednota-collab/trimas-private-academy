ABOUT TRIMAS

A school where every learner matters.

Trimas Private Academy provides primary education for learners from Standard 1 to Standard 8, with a focus on strong academic foundations, good character and confidence.

We believe education is more than passing examinations. It is about helping children think, communicate, create, collaborate and become responsible members of their communities.
